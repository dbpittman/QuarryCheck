NL Quarry Pre-Screen

Runs the acceptance checks from Sections E and G of the provincial Quarry Permit/Lease Application (MLD-Q-QP-A) against the province's own published map data, live at the moment you run it. It reports what the published record shows. It certifies nothing. All measurements are referenced to NAD83(CSRS), the provincial survey datum; each source's datum has been independently verified or corrected (see Datum audit in results).

SETBACKS: PROPERTY 15m · TRAIL 15m · ROAD 50m · PROTECTED RD 90m · WATER 50m · WETLAND 30m

Drop your boundary file here, or click to choose

KMZ, KML, or zipped shapefile — the same file the application requires. Include the access road.

Every result above is stamped with its source and query time. Sources are the Government of NL's own published services (dnrmaps Geoscience Atlas, GNL ArcGIS Online, Crown Lands Land Use Atlas) plus bundled snapshots of the Municipal Affairs protected-road KMZs. Archaeology and rare-species data are not public by design and are never screenable here. Cabin access trails are not mapped in any provincial layer. This tool holds no data and stores nothing you upload; screening runs entirely in your browser.
